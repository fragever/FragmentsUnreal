# Code of Conduct

We are committed to making participation in FragmentsUnreal welcoming,
respectful, and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background,
identity, experience, disability, nationality, or religion.

Be constructive, assume good faith, respect different technical perspectives,
give feedback on ideas rather than people, and protect confidential project and
model information.

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, sexualized
language or imagery, doxxing, and publishing private information without
permission are not acceptable.

Report concerns privately to the maintainers through the private security
contact configured in the repository's GitHub settings. Reports will be
reviewed fairly and confidentially where possible. Maintainers may remove
comments, close issues, or restrict participation to protect the community.
